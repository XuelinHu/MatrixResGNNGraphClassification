\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Message passing.} Modern graph learning builds on the spectral
convolution of Kipf and Welling~\cite{kipf2017gcn}. GraphSAGE introduced
inductive neighborhood aggregation~\cite{hamilton2017inductive}, graph attention
networks weighted neighbors by learned attention~\cite{velivckovic2017graph},
and the message-passing framework unified these designs for molecular property
prediction~\cite{gilmer2017neural}. The Weisfeiler--Lehman connection
established by GIN shows that injective multiset aggregation is central to the
discriminative power of this family~\cite{xu2019gin}, and surveys document the
resulting design space~\cite{wu2020comprehensive}.

\textbf{Deep GNNs and residual connectivity.} Depth is not free: over-smoothing
makes node representations converge as propagation depth
grows~\cite{li2018deeper,oono2020simple}, while over-squashing compresses
long-range information into fixed-size
messages~\cite{alon2020bottleneck,topping2021understanding}. DropEdge randomly
removes edges during training~\cite{rong2019dropedge}, Jumping Knowledge
aggregates representations across layers~\cite{xu2018representation}, and
residual or dense skip paths carry earlier states
forward~\cite{he2016deep}. Residual gated graph convolutional
networks learn how strongly historical states should be
reinjected~\cite{bresson2017residual}. These approaches reuse depth-wise state
within a single stream; our work keeps the same reuse principle but asks which
local residual neighborhoods on a branch-by-layer grid should be connected.

\textbf{Multi-branch and higher-order architectures.} Higher-order neighborhood
mixing improves over single-hop aggregation~\cite{abu2019mixhop}. Hierarchical
pooling adds a second axis of structure by coarsening graphs during
processing~\cite{ying2018hierarchical}, and a recent survey
catalogues these designs~\cite{li2024graph}. Such work increases the expressive
or aggregational capacity of the propagation layer. In contrast, we hold the
propagation operator fixed and vary only how hidden states are reused, so that
the residual topology itself becomes the identifiable factor.
